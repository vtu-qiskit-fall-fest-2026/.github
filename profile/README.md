## 🏛️ About Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)

**Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)**, headquartered in Belagavi, Karnataka, is one of India's premier and largest technological universities. Named after the legendary engineer and statesman **Sir M. Visvesvaraya**, VTU governs technical education across Karnataka with over 200 affiliated engineering colleges, regional centers, and hundreds of thousands of engineering students.

VTU serves as an epicenter for technological advancement, fostering industry-academia partnerships, Centers of Excellence, and cutting-edge skill initiatives in emerging technologies.

## ⚛️ About Quantum Club VTU Belagavi

**Quantum Club VTU** is the official student and researcher community at VTU dedicated to democratizing quantum computing education and hands-on skill development. 

Aligning with India's **National Quantum Mission (NQM)** and global quantum research standards, the club bridges theoretical quantum physics with practical quantum software development. It equips engineering students across disciplines with real-world experience on state-of-the-art quantum hardware and open-source frameworks.

### 🎯 Key Objectives & Focus Areas
- 🏫 **Quantum Skill Development:** Conducting hands-on workshops, bootcamps, and minor degree curriculum explorations in quantum computing.
- 🔬 **Industry & Hardware Access:** Leveraging IBM Quantum cloud platforms, Qiskit Runtime primitives, and simulators for hardware-bound research.
- 🤝 **Community & Open Source:** Encouraging student-led open-source contributions through events like the **Qiskit Fall Fest 2026**.
- 🏆 **Hackathons & Real-World Tracks:** Building solutions in Quantum Machine Learning (QML), Optimization (QAOA), Cryptography, and Quantum Chemistry (VQE).

## ⚡ Code Sample: Executing a Quantum Circuit with Qiskit v1.x

Here is how participants set up and run a Bell State circuit using `qiskit-ibm-runtime`:

```python
from qiskit import QuantumCircuit
from qiskit.quantum_info import SparsePauliOp
from qiskit_ibm_runtime import QiskitRuntimeService, EstimatorV2 as Estimator

# 1. Construct Quantum Circuit (Entangled Bell State)
qc = QuantumCircuit(2)
qc.h(0)         # Apply Hadamard gate on Qubit 0
qc.cx(0, 1)     # Apply CNOT gate (Control: 0, Target: 1)

# 2. Define Observable (ZZ Measurement)
observable = SparsePauliOp.from_list([("ZZ", 1)])

# 3. Connect to IBM Quantum Service
service = QiskitRuntimeService(channel="ibm_quantum")
backend = service.least_busy(operational=True, simulator=False)

# 4. Transpile & Execute via Estimator Primitive
estimator = Estimator(backend=backend)
job = estimator.run([(qc, observable)])
result = job.result()

print(f"Job ID: {job.job_id()}")
print(f"Expectation Value: {result[0].data.evs}")
