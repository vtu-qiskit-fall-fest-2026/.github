### 👋 Who We Are
Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Qiskit Fall Fest 2026** hosted at **Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi**! We bring together curious minds, creative builders, and the next generation of quantum thinkers across a 5-day hybrid festival (2 days online + 3 days in-person).

---

### 🌐 Hackathon & Participant Track Tiers
Our hackathon brings together dozens of innovative teams working on Qiskit algorithms, variational quantum solvers, and quantum circuit simulations. 

* **💻 Online Track (Oct 27–28):** Virtual hacking, remote environment setup with Qiskit SDK, and initial circuit implementations.
* **🏛️ In-Person Track (Oct 29–31):** Advanced hardware talks, workshops, and final project presentations on campus at VTU Belagavi.

> *All participant submissions and project code repositories are hosted right here under this organization prefixed with `vtu-hc-*`.*
